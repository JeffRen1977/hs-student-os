The course platform for high school students

High School Student OS

Every course runs on one shared engine, and every student gets a tutor that gives feedback and plans their study.

1 · Author

Brings the materials

Skills, questions, answer keys and rubrics, as data. Never touches our code.

2 · Reviewer

Checks quality

Reviews content, license and test results, then approves or rejects.

3 · Admin

Publishes the course

Puts the approved course in the catalog, open to every student.

4 · Student + tutor

Studies with a tutor

Gets feedback on every answer and a plan across all their courses.
